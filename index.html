Struggle · Transition · Resolution

An emotional journey through three rooms, designed as a museum of emotions.

Room 1 · Struggle

Whispers and ghostly moans surround the visitor. I placed them throughout the room to evoke the self-judgment and fear I felt before my emotional transition.

Room 2 · Transition

Howling winds fill the space. Their raw, unpredictable movement reflects the discomfort of change and the push and pull of moving forward while feeling unsure.

Room 3 · Resolution

Forest ambience, birdsong, and gentle water by a pond evoke peace. The sounds come together as I emerge from the struggle feeling stronger, calmer, and more in tune with myself.
