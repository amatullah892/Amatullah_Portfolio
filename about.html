Hi, I’m Amatullah.

I was born in Dubai and moved to Melbourne to pursue a bachelor’s degree in Digital Design at RMIT University.

My goal is to become a UX designer. I’m interested in understanding where people get stuck and turning what I learn into digital experiences that feel clear and easy to use.

How I work

Open each part of my practice to see how I approach it.

Research & product strategy

I use interviews and journey mapping to understand what people do, where they get stuck, and what a product should help them do next.

Interaction design & prototyping

I turn findings into flows and prototypes, then use feedback to test the decisions that matter most.

UI & design systems

I carry the work through to legible interfaces, accessible interactions, and reusable visual patterns.

Sound, motion & creative technology

Working in audio, video, and 3D gives me another way to explore how a digital experience feels and moves.
